BATTLE TACTICS

Master combat and dominate your rivals

⚔️ ARMY COMPOSITION

Balanced Force

Standard army setup for most situations

  • • 3x Infantry (front line)
  • • 2x Ranged units (damage dealers)
  • • 1x Support unit (healer/engineer)
  • • 1x Cavalry (flanking)

Aggressive Push

Offensive-focused for rapid conquest

  • • 4x Cavalry units
  • • 2x Melee infantry
  • • 2x Siege weapons
  • • Skip support for raw power

🗺️ TERRAIN BONUSES

🏔️ Hills

+20% combat bonus for defenders

Use ranged units on hills for maximum effect

🌲 Forest

+15% combat bonus, blocks cavalry

Excellent for defensive infantry lines

🏰 Fortifications

+40% combat bonus

Build forts on strategic chokepoints

🌊 Rivers

-20% combat for river crossers

Defend at rivers, attack across them

🏜️ Desert

-10% movement, no healing

Bring extra supply units

❄️ Tundra

-10% production speed

Plan for slower expansion

💡 COMBAT TIPS

Flanking

Attack enemies from sides/back for +10% damage. Use cavalry to circle around.

Zone of Control

Enemies entering adjacent tiles trigger attacks. Use to trap opponents.

Embark Wisely

Naval transport is slow and vulnerable. Escort with naval units.

Siege First

Bring siege units against cities. Walls add massive defense bonus.
